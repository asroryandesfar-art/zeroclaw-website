HOW IT WORKS

Three steps
for every order.

  1. 01

    Create a link or QR

    Choose Create Payment for an order. You get a Solana Pay link with a unique reference, and can save it as a QR image.

    ↗
  2. 02

    Your customer pays

    The customer opens the link or scans the QR in their Solana wallet and pays in SOL or USDC. Funds go directly to your wallet.

    ↗
  3. 03

    Check the status

    Choose Check Payment Status. ZeroClaw reads the blockchain and reports whether the order is still pending or paid.

    ↗
OPEN-SOURCE CORE · DEVNET
# Build the open-source core (no token needed)
$ cargo build --release --locked
$ ZSP=./target/release/solpay

# 1. Create a payment link for one order (1 USDC)
$ $ZSP create-url --amount 1 --token USDC --order-id ORDER-1

#    Save it as a QR image
$ $ZSP render-qr --url "$PAYMENT_URL" --out invoice.png

# 2. Your customer scans the QR and pays

# 3. Check the payment status
$ $ZSP settle --reference "$REFERENCE"
{ "status": "pending", "newly_settled": false }
Set your public Devnet wallet first; the open-source core README lists every step. The Ubuntu app does the same from a menu.

FAQ

Good
to know.

Who is this for?+

Shops and developers who want to accept SOL or USDC. The Ubuntu app runs in a terminal window from your app menu; developers can also build the open-source core. It is not a hosted checkout page.

Do you hold my money or my keys?+

No. Your customer pays from their own wallet straight to your wallet. ZeroClaw only needs your public wallet address and reads the blockchain to check the payment.

Does it support Solana Mainnet?+

Supports Solana Mainnet. The payment workflow was tested on Solana Devnet. Devnet is the default; switch to Mainnet in Settings after adding your own Mainnet RPC endpoint. Run a small test payment before you take live orders.

What do I need to run it?+

Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, 64-bit (amd64), an internet connection to activate, and your public wallet address. Mainnet also needs your own Solana RPC endpoint.

Is there a free version?+

Yes. The open-source core (MIT or Apache-2.0) is free and needs no token. You build and run it yourself from the command line. The US$200 license buys the ready-to-install Ubuntu app.

Does the license expire?+

No. The version you receive keeps working. Updates are included for 12 months and technical support for 90 days.
