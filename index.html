01 — THE PRODUCT

Payment signals are easy.
Payment proof is harder.

ZeroClaw Solana Pay gives agent workflows a structured way to issue requests, examine chain evidence, and record a one-time settlement decision.

/ 01

Reference-bound invoices

Every request carries a unique reference and locked payment terms, giving the verifier a specific invoice to evaluate.

/ 02

Evidence-first verification

Checks signature, finality, transfer instruction, destination, amount, mint, and invoice window before settlement.

/ 03

Durable decisions

A SQLite invoice ledger uses atomic signature claims and audit events to support repeat calls without double settlement.

02 — ASSET VERIFICATION

Built for the details
that make a payment real.

Two assets. One principle: a successful transfer must match the invoice on chain before the ledger can mark it paid.

◎ SOL
NATIVE TRANSFER

Native SOL, verified at the instruction level.

Requires a successful finalized transaction, the merchant wallet as recipient, enough lamports, a balance increase, and the invoice reference on the transfer instruction.

  • Signature and network identity
  • System transfer, recipient, and lamports
  • Reference, timing, and replay protection
$ USDC
SPL TOKEN

Canonical USDC, not a lookalike token.

Checks the approved mint, legacy Token Program, merchant-owned associated token account, six-decimal base-unit amount, and final transaction evidence.

  • Canonical mint and trusted decimals
  • Token program, recipient account, and owner
  • Reference, timing, and replay protection

The parser and decision paths have automated tests and public Mainnet read-only checks. A real paid Mainnet invoice is still required before production claims.

04 — THE FLOW

From request to
recorded settlement.

A clear sequence for an agent-assisted checkout. Confirmation comes from blockchain evidence and an atomic ledger claim, never from a chat message or screenshot.

  1. 01

    Create an invoice

    Lock the order ID, asset, amount, merchant recipient, and expiry; generate a unique reference.

    ↗
  2. 02

    Share payment instructions

    Give the customer a Solana Pay URL or QR to open in their own wallet.

    ↗
  3. 03

    Read finalized chain evidence

    Inspect the matching signature, transfer instruction, recipient, asset, amount, and reference.

    ↗
  4. 04

    Claim settlement once

    Move the invoice to paid only after verification; SQLite rejects reuse of the same signature.

    ↗
  5. 05

    Respond through your agent

    Use the typed integration result to notify the customer. Live WhatsApp delivery remains to be validated.

    ↗

A Rust CLI handles invoice and settlement decisions. A typed MCP bridge provides a structured path for ZeroClaw v0.8.3 integration without shell interpolation.

  • 01 Stable machine-readable responses
  • 02 Persisted invoice terms and order IDs
  • 03 Bounded RPC search and failure handling

Direct adapter contract tests have passed; actual WhatsApp routing and notifications have not been verified live.

INTEGRATION / ILLUSTRATIVE
# Issue a registered invoice
$ solpay create-url \
  --token USDC \
  --amount 25 \
  --order-id ORDER_001

# Verify and claim a finalized payment
$ solpay settle \
  --reference <invoice-reference>

# Only a new valid claim can return:
{ "status": "paid", "newly_settled": true }
Requires operator configuration and a live payment test before production use.

07 — FAQ

The important
questions, answered.

Clear limits are part of trustworthy payment infrastructure.

Is ZeroClaw Solana Pay production ready on Mainnet?+

Not yet. Automated tests and public Mainnet read-only verification have passed, but an actual SOL and USDC Mainnet invoice has not been paid and settled end to end. A controlled pilot and operational checks are required.

Do you hold customer funds or private keys?+

No. The customer signs from their own wallet, and the merchant receives payment directly. The verifier reads transaction evidence and records settlement; it does not request or store customer private keys.

Does the lifetime license expire after 12 months?+

No. The right to use an obtained version is permanent. The 12-month period covers new versions, and the 90-day period covers technical support. Neither date disables the installed software.

How does the commercial offer relate to open source?+

The existing Rust repository remains MIT OR Apache-2.0. The $200 offer adds a signed commercial distribution plus update and support entitlements; it does not take away previously granted open-source rights.

Is live WhatsApp checkout already verified?+

No. The typed ZeroClaw adapter has automated contract tests, while live WhatsApp routing, sender authorization, delivery, and recovery still need validation.
